# Psychodynamic Lens (CCRT)

## Overview
A cautious psychodynamic reading of a client's corpus as a set of hypotheses, built on **CCRT** (Luborsky): Wish / Response of Other / Response of Self, plus defenses and transference hints. Core principle: inference from text without nonverbal cues, free association, or history is **guessing from a transcript, not psychodynamic diagnosis** — so confidence is LOW by default and defenses are named only at a coarse LEVEL grain, never as specific psychoanalytic mechanisms.

## When to use
Use when you want a psychodynamic reading of one client's anonymized sessions. NOT for: distortion counting, protocol adherence, diagnosis, or risk scoring. Run a safety-boundary scan first.

## Input
All anonymized sessions for one client.

## Steps
Operate ONLY on already-anonymized text. Inference is cloud unless run on a local model; if cloud, files must already be redacted and human-reviewed.

1. **CCRT** — formulate the client's **Wish**, the typical **Response of Other** (expected/perceived), and the **Response of Self**, each with quotes across sessions.
2. **Defenses at LEVEL grain only** — name the coarse level of defense (avoidance of affect / intellectualization / minimization) with markers and locations. Do NOT attribute specific mechanisms in a strict psychoanalytic sense; the text does not support fine attribution.
3. **Transference hints** — feelings toward the therapist that may echo another significant figure. Mark as weak hypotheses. Do not invent countertransference — a transcript is speech, not the therapist's reflection on their own feelings.
4. **Defended material** — the avoidance theme as possibly protected material; say what *might* sit behind the steering-away, then stop. No reconstruction.

For EACH observation use the mandatory schema:

> Доказательство (цитата) → Интерпретация → Альтернатива → Уверенность (высокая/средняя/низкая) → Действие → Граница.

Analysis output language: **Russian**. Confidence LOW by default; everything is for supervision. Confidence in words, not percentages.

## Example
On `sessions-ru/client-a` (Марина): CCRT — Wish: to be loved unconditionally without earning it; Response of Other: I'll be judged insufficient and cut off; Response of Self: hypervigilance, pleasing, scanning faces, distancing. Coarse defenses: intellectualization («режим аналитик»), minimization («он из вежливости кивнул»), avoidance of affect (отец отводится 4 раза). Transference hint (weak): S3 «вы сейчас как будто туда же» — therapist briefly cast as the not-understanding other; repaired. Confidence: low throughout.

## Safety & limits
Exploratory, low-confidence, hypotheses-only — the most interpretive lens in the package. No diagnoses, no suicide-risk scoring, no treatment decisions, no reconstruction of personal history, no claims of "measuring" transference. Defenses stay at coarse level grain. These are weak hypotheses strictly for supervision. Borrowing construct definitions (CCRT/Luborsky), not reproducing psychometrics (this is NOT a DMRS defense score).
